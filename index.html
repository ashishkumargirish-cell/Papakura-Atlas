1842

Documented facts

Location: Papakura is a town on the shores of the Pahurehure inlet of the Manukau Harbour.
Distance: Te Ara describes Papakura as 34 km south of Auckland city.
Military: Papakura Military Camp was established on the outskirts in 1939; it was closed from 1992 to 2002.
Marae: Papakura Marae was established as an incorporated society in 1980.
Current local-board area: Auckland Council says the Papakura Local Board area includes Drury, Alfriston, Takanini, Hingaia, Red Hill, Pahurehure and the current Papakura town centre.
